Privacy Policy: Take a Breath

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Take a Breath, made by Studio Linicro, does not collect personal information. It does not store any on a server and does not send it anywhere.

  • The app has no accounts, sign-in or profiles of its own.
  • Your kit checklist, emergency card and household notes are saved only on your own device. They are never uploaded to us or to any server. The app does not encrypt this data. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photo library.
  • The app works fully offline. If you choose a book, phone or email link, that opens outside the app (for example Amazon, Phone or Mail).

Sharing and restore

If you choose to export a backup, kit list, walkthrough or emergency card, Take a Breath opens the standard iOS share sheet with a file. Where it goes is up to you. The app sends nothing by itself. Restore uses the system file picker; the file you pick stays on your device.

Medical information

Take a Breath is a companion to a first-aid book. It is not a medical device and it does not collect health-clinic or account data. Guidance you look up in the app is for reference only and is not a substitute for professional care or emergency services.

Children

Take a Breath collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of this, this page will be updated first and the change explained here in plain language.
