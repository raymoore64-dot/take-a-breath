Take a Breath Support

Take a Breath is a companion to the book Take A Breath. Then Act. — Home First Aid for All by Raymond Moore, RN. It is made by Studio Linicro for iPhone.

Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email DialInnStudio26@protonmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. If something went wrong, telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

About the app

The app helps you look up home first-aid guidance from the book, keep a kit checklist, and save a simple emergency card and household notes on your phone. It is not a medical device and it is not a substitute for professional care or emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?No. Take a Breath has no accounts and works fully offline. Opening an Amazon, phone or email link will leave the app.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?No.

Where is my data saved?Your kit checklist, emergency card and household notes stay only on this phone. They are not uploaded and the app does not encrypt them. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I export or restore my notes?Use Download backup, or the kit, walkthrough and emergency-card export buttons, to open the iOS share sheet and save a file where you choose. Restore uses the system file picker to bring a backup back onto the phone.

Does the app use my camera, photos or location?No. Those features are not in the iOS app.

Does the app make emergency calls or send reminders?No. Calling is not connected in this app, and it does not send reminders.

Does the app recommend medicines?No. This app does not make medicine recommendations.

Where can I get the book?The paperback is on Amazon UK and Amazon US (ASIN B0HK4MC4PZ, ISBN 979-8174990227).

Which devices are supported?iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
